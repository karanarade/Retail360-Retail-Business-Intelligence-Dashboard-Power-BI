## 📌 Project Overview

**Retail360** is an interactive **Retail Business Intelligence
dashboard** developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The project analyzes retail business performance across **sales,
customers, products, profitability, and geographical markets**. It
transforms retail data into interactive KPIs and visualizations that
help identify business trends and performance drivers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Business Objectives

-   Monitor overall sales and profitability
-   Analyze monthly sales and order trends
-   Identify top-performing products and categories
-   Understand customer behavior and retention
-   Compare performance across customer segments
-   Analyze regional, country, and city-level performance
-   Track profit margins and key business KPIs
-   Provide an interactive view for business decision-making

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a consolidated view of the organization's overall performance.

**Key KPIs:** - Gross Sales - Net Sales - Total Profit - Profit Margin -
Average Order Value - Total Orders

**Key Visuals:** - Monthly Net Sales Trend - Global Net Sales
Distribution - Net Sales by Category - Top 10 Products by Net Sales -
Net Sales & Profit by Market

![Executive Overview](Screenshots/Executive_Overview.png)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2. Sales Analysis

Focuses on sales performance, growth, products, segments, and order
trends.

**Key KPIs:** - Gross Sales - Net Sales - YoY Growth - MoM Growth -
Average Order Value - Total Orders

**Key Visuals:** - Monthly Net Sales vs Gross Sales - Top 10 Products by
Net Sales - Net Sales by Segment - Monthly Order Trend - Net & COGS by
Category

![Sales Analysis](Screenshots/Sales_Analysis.png)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3. Customer Analysis

Provides insights into customer acquisition, retention, segmentation,
and purchasing behavior.

**Key KPIs:** - Total Customers - Net Sales - Repeat Customers -
Customer Retention % - Top Customer - New Customers

**Key Visuals:** - Net Sales by Customer Segment - Net Sales vs New
Customers Trend - Customer Retention Rate Trend - Top 10 Customers by
Net Sales - Customer Distribution by Country - Customer Count by Segment

![Customer Analysis](Screenshots/Customer_Analysis.png)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 4. Product Analysis

Analyzes product sales, quantity sold, profitability, categories, and
sub-categories.

**Key KPIs:** - Total Products - Net Sales - Total Quantity Sold -
Profit Margin - Average Profit per Product - Best-Selling Product

**Key Visuals:** - Quantity Sold by Category - Monthly Quantity Sold
Trend - Net Sales by Category - Top 10 Sub-Categories by Profit vs Net
Sales vs Cost - Top 10 Products by Quantity Sold

![Product Analysis](Screenshots/Product_Analysis.png)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 5. Geographical Analysis

Analyzes sales and profitability across countries, regions, markets, and
cities.

**Key KPIs:** - Total Countries - Net Sales - Total Cities - Top Country
by Net Sales - Top Country by Profit - Total Profit

**Key Visuals:** - Net Sales by Market - Regional Net Sales, Profit &
Profit Margin - Top 5 Cities by Net Sales

![Geographical Analysis](Screenshots/Geographical_Analysis.png)

## 💡 Business Insights

-   The dashboard shows **\$11.30M in Net Sales** and approximately
    **\$1.47M in Total Profit**.
-   **Technology** is the leading category by net sales.
-   The **Consumer segment** contributes the largest share of
    customer-segment sales.
-   The **United States** is identified as the top country by both net
    sales and profit.
-   **New York City** is the leading city among the displayed top cities
    by net sales.
-   Monthly sales and quantity trends provide visibility into seasonal
    changes in business performance.
-   Product-level analysis helps identify high-performing products and
    sub-categories.
